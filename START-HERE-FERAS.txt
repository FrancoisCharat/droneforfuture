F.E.R.A.S. USE CASE
The five F.E.R.A.S. capabilities now share a building-fire use case.
S — Synchronise physical & digital combines real-time sensor streams
with verified updates to asset condition, location and geometry.
Mobile sensors, including thermal drones where appropriate, can
supplement fixed coverage. Observations are located and timestamped;
missing or stale data and the last known state remain visible.

PREVIEW ON YOUR COMPUTER
1. Extract this ZIP into a NEW folder named DFF-Consulting-FERAS-v6.
2. Open website/index.html in your browser.
3. Select Approach, then S — Synchronise physical & digital.

UPLOAD TO WWW.DRONEFORFUTURE.COM
1. Back up your existing homepage and assets folder.
2. Open your domain's web root using your hosting file manager or FTP.
3. Upload website/index.html and the complete website/assets folder
   into that web root, replacing the corresponding files together.
   The homepage should be at your web root/index.html.
4. Refresh your website. If it still shows an older page, clear the
   host/CDN cache if applicable and force-refresh the browser with
   Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows/Linux) or Command+Shift+R (macOS).

Preserve unrelated folders such as FC/ and DT1/.
See README.md for full instructions and ASSET-NOTES.md for image notes.

itself does not connect to live sensors.
